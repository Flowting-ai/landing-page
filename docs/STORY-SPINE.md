# Story Spine — the narrative every Home section inherits

> Seed for the `visual-director` loop. The metaphor + emotional arc the page rides. The Hero has no
> incoming handoff — it **establishes** the spine; this file is its "from." Each section's handoff
> (in `docs/visuals/<section>.md`) refines the per-section beats below as the loop runs.

## The spine (one paragraph)
**Souvenir is the memory your work keeps.** Your tools, context, and effort are scattered across a
dozen apps and lost between them; Souvenir unifies them into one Brain — a coordinated team of AI
assistants that remember everything and *do* the work, for you and for your team. The page should feel
like watching scattered fragments settle into a warm, kept, ordered thing. We immerse the visitor in
the chaos before we show the calm; we make the intangible orchestration *tangible*; and every beat is
unmistakably ours, never the generic SaaS rhythm.

## Emotional arc (problem-immersion → relief → expansion → proof)
chaos felt → one calm layer → the shift from manual to automatic → it's for *you* and your *team* →
your knowledge understood and acted on → why it's different → one Brain, a coordinated workforce.
