# Brand

The marketing site and the product share **one** brand. The wordmark, the mark, and the
Besley/Geist pairing must read identically across both — this is non-negotiable. All assets live in
`public/brand/` and render through `@/components/site/Logo`, a single mask-recolored SVG component
(`background: currentColor`) so the *same* files work on light and dark by swapping a text color.

## Logo lockups

`Logo` has four variants. Recolor by setting the text color (`text-ink` on light, `text-dark-ink`
on dark) — never ship a second hard-coded color file.

## On light + on dark

Same files, recolored. On light the logo is **ink** (`--ink`); on dark it's **`--dark-ink`** (warm
cream) — never pure white, to match the warm system.

## Clearspace & minimum size

- **Clearspace:** keep clear margin around the logo equal to the **height of the mark** on all sides.
  Nothing — text, edges, other logos — intrudes into it.
- **Minimum size:** lockup no smaller than **20px** tall; mark no smaller than **16px**. Below that,
  switch to the mark-only.
- The mask technique means the logo is always **monochrome** — it takes one color from
  `currentColor`. There is no multi-color logo; don't invent one.

## Do's & Don'ts

**Do** — recolor via text color · keep clearspace · use the mark in tight/square slots · pair with
Besley/Geist (see Typography).

**Don't** — ❌ recolor the logo with the **mauve accent** or any brand hue (it's ink/cream only) ·
❌ stretch, skew, rotate, or add effects (glow/shadow/outline) · ❌ place the lockup on a busy photo
without a solid plate · ❌ recreate the wordmark in a different typeface · ❌ drop below minimum size.
