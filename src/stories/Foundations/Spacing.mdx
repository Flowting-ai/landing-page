## Rules (from souvenir-learnings §4)

- **Nested radius = outer − padding.** Matching inner and outer radius leaves a visible gap.
- **`gap` on the parent** for spacing between children — no trailing margins.
- **`max-width`** on text containers to protect the measure.
- **Asymmetry over default symmetry** — it creates interest; centered-everything reads as a template.
- **Mobile:** `dvh` not `vh`; set `aspect-ratio` on media to prevent layout shift.
- Watch `overflow: hidden` — it silently breaks sticky children.
