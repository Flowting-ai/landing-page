# Share & Collaborate on Agents

Work on agents as a team — share an agent with specific teams, set visibility, and use it together inside a team project.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmqzuimqs0c24qm4i4jrtz190

## 1. To get started, click the Agents tab to view and manage your available agents.

## 2. Afterwards, click the share link button to share the agent configuration with your team.

## 3. Souvenir lets you build an agent once and share it with specific team members or your entire team — so everyone works from the same brain without losing control over who has access to what.

## 4. Then click the dropdown to select which teams in your organization can access this agent.

## 5. After that, select the team you want to share this agent with.

## 6. Clicking Save visibility makes the agent visible to the team members you selected.

## 7. Next, click the Republish button to save and deploy your sharing changes.

## 8. Next up, click the Chats tab to view and manage your conversations with agents.

## 9. Click the teams dropdown to select the team where you want to use this agent.

## 10. Scroll to find the team you've shared this agent with.

## 11. Then, click the Product-Design team to open its workspace and access the agent.

## 12. Select the project under the selected team to open it.

## 13. From here, click the attachment button to add an agent inside your team's project.

## 14. Next, click Add agent to access your agent.

## 15. Now select the agent you shared with this team — your teammates will be able to access it too.
