# Compare and Deploy AI Models on Souvenir

Run the same prompt across GPT, Claude, and Gemini side by side, then deploy the model that wins — without leaving the chat.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmqydx38s1s8oqmec5lt248dw

## 1. Firstly, click the Compare Models button to evaluate different AI model options.

## 2. Following that, click GPT-5 Mini to view its features and specifications.

## 3. From here, click the Anthropic filter to view only Anthropic models available for comparison.

## 4. Now, click on Google to view and select the Gemini 3.5 Flash model option.

## 5. Now, click the Test models button to run a comparison of your selected models.

## 6. Next up, click the message input field to ask a question about model performance.

## 7. After that, click the Send button to submit your prompt and view model results.

## 8. From here, click the expand icon to view detailed model comparison metrics and performance data.

## 9. After that, click the "Use this model" button to deploy the selected AI model.

## 10. Next up, select Claude Sonnet 4.6 to choose your preferred AI model.

## 11. From here, type your question or request in the message input field below.
