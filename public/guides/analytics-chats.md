# Move & Monitor Chats

Move chats into projects and keep your analytics work organized across your workspace.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmr01tp5n0hjwqm4ik37vggf8

## 1. Select a chat you want to move using the menu in the menu bar, then choose “Move to Projects.”

## 2. From here, click Move to project to relocate this chat to a project.

## 3. Following this, select the project you want to move your chat to.

## 4. Afterwards, click the Move to project button to confirm.

## 5. Then go back to the project where you sent your chat and select it.

## 6. Now, inside the project, you can see your chat and keep working with it.
