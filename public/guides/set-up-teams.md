# Set Up Teams

Create a team, describe what it works on, add members and roles, and scope which connectors it can use.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmr04gffk0jkjqm4ippkgw1jc

## 1. Start by clicking Manage Souvenir to access workspace and team settings.

## 2. Next up, click the Teams menu option to manage your organization's team settings.

## 3. Next up, click the Create new team button to add a new team to your workspace.

## 4. Next up, enter a team name in the text field to create your new team.

## 5. Now, enter a description in the field to explain what the team works on.

## 6. Afterwards, click “Create Team” to build the new team in Souvenir.

## 7. Following this, click Team settings to customize the team configuration.

## 8. Now, click Invite members to add new members to the team.

## 9. Now, click Select workspace members to add existing members to this team.

## 10. Next up, select the team member to add them to this team.

## 11. Afterwards, select Member or Editor to assign a role to the invited team member.

## 12. Now, click Send invites to add the selected member to your team.

## 13. After that, search for connectors by typing keywords in the search field.

## 14. Select the connectors you want to give a team access to — for example, give the Finance team access to finance connectors only, while keeping other teams restricted.
