# Create and Deploy a Custom AI Agent on Souvenir

Build a custom AI agent from scratch — give it a name, instructions, knowledge, and tools, then deploy it to your workspace.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmqykxfc71x0xqmecwmljci35

## 1. To begin, click the Agents tab to view and manage available AI agents.

## 2. This is your AI Assistant library. All your team's assistants live here. Click any card to open it or create a new one

## 3. Then, click the New agent button to create and configure a new AI agent.

## 4. Pick a template to get started quickly, or click Start blank to build a custom AI Assistant from scratch.

## 5. From here, click Start blank to create a new custom agent from scratch.

## 6. Next, enter a concise description of what this agent should accomplish in plain English.

## 7. Then, click the Continue button to proceed to the next configuration step.

## 8. Next up, enter your desired agent name in the text field to continue setup.

## 9. Now, click the Continue button to proceed with setting up your agent.

## 10. Choose how your AI Assistant should sound and behave in every conversation.

## 11. Afterwards, click the Continue button to proceed to the next configuration step.

## 12. Click Select model to choose the right AI model for your assistant. Use a cost-efficient model for simple tasks and a powerful one for complex work.

## 13. After that, select any model from the model dropdown list to configure the agent.

## 14. Review the system instruction to see how your assistant behaves. You can edit it or add your own custom instructions to tailor it to your exact needs.

## 15. Not sure how to improve your instructions? Click Enhance and Souvenir will automatically make them clearer and more powerful.

## 16. Set the creativity level. Closer to 0 means precise answers straight from your sources. Closer to 1 gives your assistant more freedom to research and go beyond the source.

## 17. Now, click the Continue button to proceed to the next configuration step.

## 18. Then, click the "Change Image" button to update the agent's avatar.

## 19. Following that, click the Continue button to proceed to the next configuration step.

## 20. Now, click Upload Files to add knowledge sources for your Agent.

## 21. After that, click the Continue button to proceed to the next configuration step.

## 22. Here you can see all the apps already connected to your workspace.

## 23. Toggle any one on to give your AI Assistant access to its data.

## 24. Need to connect a new app? Click Manage in Settings to add connectors and make them available to your AI Assistants.

## 25. After that, click the Continue button to proceed to the next step.

## 26. Next up, click Save version to preserve your current sharing configuration changes.

## 27. View your AI Assistant's full version history and restore any previous version at any time.

## 28. Next up, close the Versions panel to return to the main Sharing Configuration view.

## 29. From here, click AI Suggestions to receive automated recommendations for sharing configuration.

## 30. From here, click the input field to ask the AI for guidance on improving your agent.

## 31. From here, click the input field to ask AI for suggestions improving your agent.

## 32. Afterwards, click the send button to deliver the email invitation to your colleague.

## 33. After that, click the expand button to view AI suggestions for optimizing your agent configuration.

## 34. Next up, click the close button to dismiss the AI suggestions panel.

## 35. After that, click the Test Chat button to preview your agent in action.

## 36. Afterwards, test your agent by typing a message in the test chat field.

## 37. Next up, click the close button to exit the test chat panel.

## 38. Next up, click the Publish button to deploy your sharing configuration changes.

## 39. Now, click "Use this agent" to add the Souvenir Scout agent to your workspace.

## 40. Ask your AI Assistant anything and get answers tailored exactly to how you trained it.

## 41. Your AI Assistant can pull live data from connected apps like Shopify and ClickUp to give you real time answers and complete tasks for you.

## 42. Next up, click the Send button to submit your message to the agent.

## 43. Your AI Assistant pulls live data and gets the work done for you automatically.
