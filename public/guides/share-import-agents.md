# Share & Import Agents

Share the agents you build and import agents your teammates send you — all in a couple of clicks.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmqzn5zlk05ghqm4iiyfakry7

## 1. Firstly, click the Agents tab to view and manage your agents.

## 2. Choose an agent from your library that you want to share, then click the copy link button to copy its URL.

## 3. After that, enter your colleague's email address to send them a sharing invite.

## 4. Then, click the Send invite button to share this agent with the recipient.

## 5. Following this, click the Republish button to save and publish your sharing changes.

## 6. On the receiving end: open the email with the shared agent link and click Add to my account to import it.

## 7. After that, click the Accept & copy agent button to add the Souvenir Scout agent.

## 8. Go to your Agents page and click Use in chat to start using the agent in the chatspace.

## 9. As the final step, click the message input field to type your message to the Souvenir Scout agent.
