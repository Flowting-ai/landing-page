# Connectors & Permissions

Enable connectors for your whole organization, approve member requests, and scope shared connectors to specific teams.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmr05pgw50kgwqm4iu8qbvizy

## 1. First off, click on “Manage Souvenir” to access settings and configuration options.

## 2. Next, click the Connectors option to configure your workspace integrations.

## 3. In the connectors catalog, you can see every connector Souvenir offers.

## 4. Following this, toggle the switch to enable a connector for your entire organization — use this for a common connector everyone in your org uses.

## 5. In the Permissions tab, you can see who is requesting connector access — editors and members can request a connector for their team or project.

## 6. Next, review the connector request message and the member who requested it before approving.

## 7. After that, click the Approve button to authorize the connector request.

## 8. In the Manage Connectors section, you can manage connectors shared across multiple accounts and assign them to a specific team.

## 9. Next, click the Manage button to configure shared connector accounts.

## 10. After that, click Manage to configure the connector settings and team access.

## 11. Enable this toggle to attach the shared connector to a specific team.

## 12. Now you have the flexibility to grant connector permissions at the individual team level.

## 13. Now, click the All permissions dropdown to view the available permission options.
