# Organize Pins & Folders

Keep your best chats within reach — pin what matters, add notes, and group your pins into folders.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmqycwqt51rriqmec7zntp78u

## 1. Click the pin button to save this response for later.

## 2. Your pinned response is saved here. Click to expand and read the full content.

## 3. Click here to add a comment or note to this pin.

## 4. Afterwards, click the Save button to preserve your comment to the pinboard.

## 5. Following that, click the “Show in chat” button to display the pinned content within your chat conversation.

## 6. Click Insert to drop this pin directly into your chat.

## 7. From here, click Organize to arrange and structure your saved pins.

## 8. Following that, click the Organise button to manage your pins.

## 9. Following that, click the New folder button to create an organized folder structure.

## 10. Give your folder a name.

## 11. Afterwards, click the “Create folder” button to complete the new folder setup.

## 12. Select the pin you want to move into a folder.

## 13. Then, click “Move to folder” to relocate the selected pin.

## 14. From here, select Market Research to move your pin to that folder.

## 15. Then, click the Done button to complete your selection and close the dialog.

## 16. Next up, click Market Research to view and organize pins in that folder.

## 17. Now, click the close button to exit the expanded pinboard view.

## 18. Next, click the close button to dismiss the pinboard panel and return to chat.

## 19. Next, click the “New chat” button to start a fresh conversation.

## 20. Click here and ask anything. Type @ to instantly search and pull in your saved pins and past conversations.

## 21. Following that, click the X button to remove the attached pin tag.

## 22. Click here to attach your entire pins folder to the chat.

## 23. Now, click Pin folders to save your frequently used folders for quick access.

## 24. As the last step, select Market Research from the dropdown menu to choose your folder.
