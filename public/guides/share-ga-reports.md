# Share GA Reports

Turn an analytics chat into a shareable report — set its visibility, choose a permission level, and share it with the right people.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmr02l5h90idpqm4iv1q91biq

## 1. Firstly, click the Google Analytics Two-Week Summary chat to open it.

## 2. Next, click the Share chat button to export or share this conversation.

## 3. Now, click the Private option to keep this chat visible only to you.

## 4. Following that, click the “Read only” dropdown to select the appropriate sharing permission level.

## 5. Afterwards, select Read only from the dropdown to set chat access permissions.

## 6. Following that, click the “Select person…” dropdown to choose a recipient for sharing.

## 7. Following this, select a person from the dropdown to share the chat directly with them.

## 8. Following this, click the person you want to select for chat sharing.

## 9. From here, click the Share button to send this chat to specific people or teams.

## 10. To end, click the close button to dismiss the Share chat dialog.
