# The Souvenir Chatspace

Your home base for working with AI — start a chat, switch models on the fly, and keep every conversation in one place.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmqyb17cv1rdnqmecccdqaxdw

## 1. To begin, click the Chats tab to access your conversation history and messaging interface.

## 2. Click here to switch your AI model — pick the one that fits your task.

## 3. Select Muse for everyday tasks. It's fast, smart, and uses fewer credits.

## 4. Next, click the dropdown menu to select a different AI Muse model.

## 5. Select Advanced for complex or high-stakes tasks — best quality, no compromises.

## 6. Then, enter your question or request in the message input field to chat.

## 7. Finally, click the send button to submit your message to the agent.
