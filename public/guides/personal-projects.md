# Personal Projects

Keep your own work in its own space — switch to personal projects and spin up a new one with a name and goals.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmr02g0w70i76qm4ibhrzrpw8

## 1. First, click the Chats tab to view and manage your conversations.

## 2. Next, click the dropdown arrow to switch between personal projects and team projects.

## 3. After that, click Personal projects to view and manage all your personal projects.

## 4. Now, click “New project” to create a fresh project within your personal workspace.

## 5. After that, enter a clear project name in the text field to identify your project.

## 6. Following that, enter your project goals in the text area to provide context.

## 7. To wrap up, click the Create project button to finalize your new project setup.
