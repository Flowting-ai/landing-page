# Highlights

Save the moments that matter — highlight key passages from any answer, reuse them, and find them all in one panel.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmqybsk6y1rfnqmecg5fxnbek

## 1. First up, click the message input field to begin typing your message.

## 2. Following this, click the Send button to submit your question to the agent.

## 3. Now highlight the first point so you can save it for later.

## 4. Now, click the Highlight button to emphasize important text in the answer.

## 5. Click Copy to grab this highlight — paste it into any chat or app later.

## 6. Now paste your copied highlight here and ask a follow-up question.

## 7. Afterwards, click Open in chat to view the highlighted content in the main conversation.

## 8. Don't need it anymore? Click the delete icon to remove the highlight.

## 9. Now, click the Close button to dismiss the highlight panel and continue.

## 10. Open the Highlights panel here to find every passage you've saved, all in one place.
