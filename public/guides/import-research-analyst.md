# Import the Research Analyst

Add the ready-made Google Research Analyst agent to your workspace from a teammate's share link.

Interactive demo: https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmqyewcr81sjbqmecahai2272

## 1. Open the email your teammate sent you, find the shared agent, and click Add to my account to import it into your workspace.

## 2. Then, click “Accept & copy agent” to add the Google Research Analyst to your account.

## 3. To end off, click the Shared tab to view agents shared with your team.
