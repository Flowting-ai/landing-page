What this means for Souvenir

The dominant pattern: confident hero text (centered or asymmetric) + a real, beautifully-framed product visual below — very often with tabs to switch product areas. Nobody ships an abstract wired diagram as the hero; they show the actual product. (That's a strike against my Variant C.)

The recommended direction for us: Anthropic's warmth (cream + Besley, asymmetric headline, editorial dark cards) + ElevenLabs/Attio's tabbed product showcase — built from real KDS components (MessageBubble, ChartCard, Sidebar-as-chrome). Tabs map straight onto your Figma structure: Chatspace · Brain · Slack.

This keeps the identity you approved, fixes "generic/plain" with depth + a real product showcase, reuses the design system, and matches the proven pattern. We can still drop one dark editorial section (Resend/Linear mood) for drama.
